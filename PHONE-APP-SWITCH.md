# Switch your phone off the old CVHC app (2 minutes)

The old installed app (the one side-loaded onto phones in June) is frozen at an
old version — it demands GPS before it lets you clock in and will never update
itself. The website version is current and clock-in always works, even when GPS
is slow. Do this once on each caregiver phone:

## 1. Remove the old app
1. Press and hold the **CVHC HomeCare** app icon.
2. Tap **Uninstall** (or drag to Uninstall) and confirm.

*(If pressing the icon only shows "Remove from Home Screen", it's already a
shortcut, not the old app — skip to step 2.)*

## 2. Add the current app to your home screen
1. Open **Chrome** and go to the CRM login page (same address as always).
2. Tap the **⋮ menu** (top right) → **"Add to Home screen"** (may say
   **"Install app"**).
3. Tap **Add**. A CVHC icon appears on your home screen.

That icon now always opens the up-to-date version — no reinstalling ever.

## 3. Log in and check
1. Open the new icon and log in.
2. Tap Clock In on your client — it should work immediately, even indoors.
3. If a **"🔄 A new version is available — Reload"** bar ever appears at the
   bottom, tap **Reload**. That's how updates arrive.

## If clock-in complains about Location
Only payer-billed (Medicaid/VA) clients require Location. If you see the
"Location permission is OFF" message: tap the **🔒 padlock** in Chrome's address
bar → **Permissions** → **Location** → **Allow**, and make sure the phone's own
Location toggle is on. Then try again.
